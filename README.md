## About Me

<div align="center">
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Specialization-JavaScript_Full--Stack-F7DF1E?style=for-the-badge&logo=javascript&logoColor=black" alt="JS Full Stack" />

<br/>

I am a **JavaScript full-stack engineer** — React and Next.js on the frontend, Node.js and Express on the backend, with PostgreSQL and MongoDB underneath. I am a final-year **Information Technology student at the University of Rwanda, School of ICT**, and I am sharpening that specialization further through a two-year full-stack engineering bootcamp at **THE GYM Rwanda**.

I build software at the intersection of practical infrastructure and applied AI: localized language assistants, offline-first data sync, and resilient full-stack architecture for markets where connectivity and access are not guaranteed.

I currently co-found and build two products end to end, frontend to database:

- **Rosha AI** a WhatsApp-first, Kinyarwanda-language AI tax compliance assistant for Rwandan SMEs.
- **Sanara** a patient health record system built for offline sync in low-connectivity clinical environments.

<br/>

## Experience

**Co-Founder**  Rosha AI · Sanara
